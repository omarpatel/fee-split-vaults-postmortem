==============================================================================
SECTION 9 PILOT - PRE-REGISTERED ANALYSIS PLAN
pilot pilot-2026-q4   plan vault-pilot-plan/v1
==============================================================================

This document is committed by hash before the first block is observed. The
analysis recomputes that hash and refuses to run if the plan moved. Everything
below - the endpoint, the exclusions, the power derivation, the decision rule
and the words that will be published in each outcome - is fixed at the moment
of commitment and is not available afterwards.

It is generated from the analysis plan itself rather than written next to it,
so the document and the enforcing code cannot disagree.

==============================================================================
1. HYPOTHESIS
==============================================================================

Staffing the desk during a 12-hour block raises quote-denominated Venue-pool
creator fees credited in the following 12 hours.

This is the gate for the operations component of the service, which carries
roughly 45% of its claimed value. If the effect is not demonstrated, that
component is withdrawn from the offer rather than re-argued.

==============================================================================
2. DESIGN
==============================================================================

  kind                     within_coin_time_randomized
  total length             60 days
  block length             12 hours
  randomized phase         first 30 days
  second half              full coverage for every coin, every block
  coins enrolled           40 to 60
  matched pairs per coin   30
  price to the client      free of charge; no fee, no budget, no vault, no contract

The draw is taken at the DAY level. Each randomized day is a matched pair of
two 12-hour blocks and exactly one of them is staffed, so the desk is never
away for more than 24 consecutive hours and hour-of-day balances exactly
rather than in expectation. Every coin receives full coverage for the second
half: the study randomizes WHEN a free service arrives, never WHETHER it
arrives.

The assignment is a pure function of a seed published before enrolment, so any
third party can recompute every arm of every block from the seed alone.

==============================================================================
3. ENDPOINTS
==============================================================================

  [PRIMARY] fees_log1p
      log1p of quote-denominated Venue-pool creator fees (USD, pinned price)
      credited in the 12 hours following the block.
      transform log1p   window following_block_span

  [SECONDARY] unique_new_buyers
      Unique addresses making their first ever buy in the following 12 hours.
      transform identity   window following_block_span

  [SECONDARY] telegram_net_join_rate
      Telegram (joins - leaves) divided by end-of-window members.
      transform rate   window following_block_span

  [SECONDARY] unanswered_backlog
      Sum of the Digest topic code unanswered_backlog in the following 12
      hours. Digest-derived only; no raw message is ever read.
      transform identity   window following_block_span

Every endpoint is measured over the 12 hours FOLLOWING the block, which is the
next block's own span. That overlap is a property of the design and not an
oversight; the next block's arm is recorded as a diagnostic so the carryover
can be described, and it is not a covariate.

The secondaries carry no multiplicity control and cannot support the decision.
They are reported because a positive primary with contradictory secondaries is
worth knowing about, not because any of them can rescue a negative primary.

==============================================================================
4. STRATIFICATION AND COVARIATES
==============================================================================

  hour_of_day              stratification
  day_of_week              stratification

Adjustment covariates: NONE, and the analysis refuses any covariate that is
not one of the stratification variables above. Without that refusal,
hour-of-day and day-of-week stop being stratification variables and become the
two knobs a motivated analyst turns until the desk looks profitable.

==============================================================================
5. EXCLUSIONS
==============================================================================

Each rule below names the physical mechanism it removes. All of them are
applied by the analysis function itself, from this committed plan, so none can
be forgotten and none can be added later. The analysis refuses to run if an
exclusion report supplied with a dataset does not match the set this plan
produces.

  NON_RANDOMIZED_PHASE
  ----------------------------------------------------------------------------
    MECHANISM
      Every coin receives full desk coverage for the second half of the trial.
      Those blocks were never randomized.
    RULE
      A block whose phase is not RANDOMIZED contributes nothing to the primary
      comparison.
    JUSTIFICATION
      There is no contrast to make. The full-coverage half exists so that the
      study randomizes WHEN a free service arrives and never WHETHER it
      arrives.

  UNVALUED_WINDOW
  ----------------------------------------------------------------------------
    MECHANISM
      Approved quote assets are upgradeable, pausable proxies. A paused asset,
      an unapproved asset or a missing pinned price means the window has no
      USD value.
    RULE
      A window whose fees could not be valued is excluded. Its endpoint is
      recorded as NaN, never as zero, so a failure to exclude it aborts the
      analysis instead of silently averaging in a false zero.
    JUSTIFICATION
      An unmeasured window is not a zero-fee window. Imputing zero would move
      the endpoint toward zero in whichever arm happened to be running when
      the asset was paused, and the pause has no relationship to the desk.

  LAUNCH_WINDOW_ANTI_SNIPE
  ----------------------------------------------------------------------------
    MECHANISM
      Venue charges an anti-snipe tax of 99% at t=0 that decays to zero over
      roughly three seconds, and it reaches the fee recipient along the
      ordinary fee rails. It is real money, it is credited like ordinary
      revenue, and relative to the organic 12-hour flow of a coin it can be
      one to two orders of magnitude larger.
    RULE
      Every follow-window that starts within 24 hours of the TokenLaunched
      timestamp for that coin is excluded, and so is any window carrying a
      credit the classifier attributed to the anti-snipe window (within 5
      seconds of launch, or an effective fee rate above twice the hook fee).
      Because the drop is at the day level, the sibling 12-hour block of the
      same day goes with it.
    JUSTIFICATION
      Three reasons for 24 hours rather than "the first block". First,
      post-graduation fees arrive as AGGREGATED SWEEPS whose timestamp is that
      of the sweep and not of the trade, so a windfall accrued at t=0 can be
      credited materially later and a one-block rule would miss it. Second,
      the same first day carries launch price discovery, which is a different
      mechanism with the same signature: an enormous fee spike that no desk
      caused. Third, dropping a whole 24-hour day-pair preserves the EXACT
      hour-of-day balance the assignment generator guarantees, which dropping
      a single half-day would break - and hour-of-day is a stratification
      variable precisely because this data is violently diurnal. The
      onboarding hold the operator already runs is 900 seconds, which is
      calibrated to tax decay and settlement rather than to sweep cadence, so
      it is not the right N here and 24 hours is the conservative choice on
      all three grounds.

  GRADUATION_MECHANISM_CHANGE
  ----------------------------------------------------------------------------
    MECHANISM
      Before graduation the creator fee of a coin is an explicit per-trade
      field on its own curve contract. After graduation it is an aggregated
      PoolFeesSwept keyed by poolId. The rate basis, the batching and the
      timestamp semantics all change, and the first sweep after the switch can
      carry accrued backlog.
    RULE
      Windows in which both mechanisms are observed are excluded, and so is
      every window starting within 48 hours of the first observed
      PoolFeesSwept for that coin. The day-pair widening applies. The coin
      keeps all of its other windows.
    JUSTIFICATION
      The endpoint would otherwise change definition inside the series of a
      single coin, which is the one thing a within-coin design cannot absorb.
      48 hours covers the first full sweep cycle after the change so that the
      backlog sweep is not read as a desk effect. Excluding the coin entirely
      would be worse: graduation is plausibly correlated with the traits that
      make a coin worth studying.

  RECIPIENT_CHANGE
  ----------------------------------------------------------------------------
    MECHANISM
      The creator-fee recipient can move: self-service and immediate by the
      current recipient, or by the three-day-timelocked override held by the
      Venue owner. After it moves, the fees belong to a different party under
      different incentives, and that party may be running their own promotion.
    RULE
      Every window of that coin from the recipient change onward is excluded.
      Earlier windows are kept.
    JUSTIFICATION
      The change is an intervention on the thing being measured and it is not
      caused by the desk. It is not reversible by any analysis, so the coin is
      censored at the change rather than adjusted.

  COMMITTED PARAMETERS
    launch exclusion              24 hours from launch
    graduation exclusion          48 hours from the first sweep
    anti-snipe classification     5 seconds from launch
    drop unit                     whole 24-hour day-pair
    delivery-failure ceiling      10% of staffed blocks
    whale flag threshold          50% of a window in one credit

  IN PROSE, AS THE PLAN CARRIES IT
    - Blocks in the full-coverage second half are not randomized and are
      excluded from the primary comparison.
    - A coin contributes only strata in which both arms were observed.
    - A window whose fees could not be valued (kill-switched asset, missing
      pinned price) is excluded, never imputed as zero.
    - Every follow-window starting within 24 hours of the coin launch is
      excluded, because the 99%-at-t=0 anti-snipe tax reaches the fee
      recipient along the ordinary rails and can dominate a log1p series by
      itself.
    - Windows within 48 hours of the coin graduating are excluded, because
      graduation changes the fee mechanism underneath the series of that coin.
    - Every window from a creator-fee recipient change onward is excluded for
      that coin.
    - A whale-dominated window and a block the desk failed to deliver are
      FLAGGED and kept: both are conditioned on what happened after
      randomization, and dropping either would bias the effect toward the
      operator.

==============================================================================
6. SENSITIVITY ANALYSES (REPORTED, NEVER DECISIVE)
==============================================================================

These two contaminants are deliberately NOT excluded from the primary. Both
condition on something that happens after randomization, and dropping either
would bias the effect in the direction the operator wants. They are run,
printed next to the primary, and structurally incapable of changing the
verdict.

  PER_PROTOCOL_DELIVERY   gates the decision: no
      Re-runs the primary excluding blocks where the desk did not deliver the
      assigned arm - a staffed block we failed to cover, or an unstaffed block
      somebody answered in anyway.
    WHY IT IS NOT AN EXCLUSION
      The primary is intention-to-treat. Dropping non-compliant blocks
      conditions on post-randomization behaviour and reliably flatters the
      treatment, because the blocks we fail to cover are not a random sample
      of blocks. Delivery failure is instead capped: past 10% of staffed
      blocks the trial is reported as compromised, which can only withhold a
      proceed and never grant one.

  WHALE_DOMINATED_WINDOW   gates the decision: no
      Re-runs the primary excluding windows in which a single fee credit
      accounts for at least half of the USD total for that window.
    WHY IT IS NOT AN EXCLUSION
      A single enormous trade is real revenue with no plausible desk
      mechanism, so the temptation to drop it is strong. It is nonetheless an
      outcome-conditioned exclusion applied after randomization, and even
      applied blind to arm it biases the treatment effect whenever treatment
      shifts the outcome distribution - which is the hypothesis. The log1p
      transform already compresses a whale by construction. So it is flagged,
      reported, and left in.

7. POWER
==============================================================================

n = 40-60 arrived from a brief. What follows is the derivation for THIS design
- paired within coin, matched 12-hour blocks, Hodges-Lehmann with a percentile
bootstrap, decided by the two-part rule in section 8 - and not for the
between-coin trial whose textbook formula would flatter it.

  METHOD
    normal_approximation_validated_by_simulation
    The committed rule reads only the bootstrap interval and the
    Hodges-Lehmann point estimate, both asymptotically normal, so the rule has
    a usable normal approximation and that is what the table below reports.
    The Wilcoxon signed-rank test has no closed-form power; a seeded Monte
    Carlo simulation that runs the real analysis functions supplies it, and
    validates the table at the spot-check points.

  ASSUMPTIONS, STATED SO THEY CAN BE ARGUED WITH
    alpha                              0.05
    target power                       80%
    matched pairs per coin             30
    within-coin SD grid (log1p)        0.5, 1, 1.5, 2, 2.5
    planning within-coin SD            1.5
    between-coin effect SD             0.1
    estimator efficiency vs the mean   0.9549
    coins enrolled                     40 to 60
    simulation                         seed vault-pilot-power/v1, 400 trials, 1000 bootstrap draws

    The within-coin SD is the SD of log1p(fees) across 12-hour windows after
    hour-of-day and day-of-week are taken out as strata. It must still include
    day-level shocks: the estimator compares a staffed Monday morning against
    a DIFFERENT unstaffed Monday morning, so a day-level shock is not
    differenced out by that contrast. The between-coin effect SD is
    heterogeneity in the true effect, which no number of blocks per coin
    removes - only more coins touch it.

    The two agree to within about six points at every checked design, in both

    trials. Neither is tuned to the other. Under a true null the committed
    rule returns PROCEED about 3% of the time, below alpha, because the rule
    is one-sided in effect and additionally requires the point estimate to
    clear the threshold.

  MINIMUM DETECTABLE EFFECT, at the planning SD
    40 coins   +19.88% per staffed block
    60 coins   +15.96% per staffed block

  ADEQUACY, COMMITTED IN ADVANCE
    POWER, DERIVED AND REGISTERED BEFORE COLLECTION. n=40-60 originally came
    from a brief rather than from a derivation. Derived for this design -
    paired within coin, 30 matched 12-hour pairs per coin, Hodges-Lehmann with
    a percentile bootstrap, alpha 0.05, 80% target - the answer has three
    parts, and the first is the one that matters most. (1) THE COMMITTED RULE
    CANNOT DETECT +10% AT ANY SAMPLE SIZE. The rule requires the POINT
    ESTIMATE to reach +10%, so against a true effect of exactly +10% it fires
    with probability approaching one half from below, however many coins are
    enrolled. +10% is the minimum effect worth acting on, not the smallest
    effect this trial can find. A DO NOT PROCEED result rules out a large
    effect; it does NOT rule out a real effect somewhere between zero and
    +10%, and it must never be reported as though it did. (2) AGAINST +20%,
    40-60 COINS IS ADEQUATE ONLY IF THE ENDPOINT IS NOT TOO NOISY. At a
    within-coin SD of 1.5 the minimum detectable effect is +19.9% at 40 coins
    and +16.0% at 60, and 40 coins is exactly the number needed for 80% power
    against +20%. At an SD of 1.0 the design is generous: 19 coins would do.
    At an SD of 2.0 it is INSUFFICIENT - 69 coins are needed and 60 delivers
    75% - and at an SD of 2.5 it is badly insufficient, needing 106. An SD of
    2.0 to 2.5 is not a pessimistic invention: a late-life coin whose 12-hour
    windows alternate between real flow and literally zero is zero-inflated,
    and a series that is 30% zeros and 70% around log1p 5 has an SD near 2.3.
    (3) THE HONEST CONCLUSION. Enrol at the TOP of the range, 60 and not 40,
    because the marginal coin is cheap and the difference between 40 and 60 is
    the difference between +19.9% and +16.0% detectable. Report the observed
    within-coin SD in the results, because it determines retrospectively which
    of the rows above the trial was actually in. And if the observed SD comes
    in at or above 2.0, say plainly that the trial was underpowered for its
    own question rather than reporting the null as an answer.

==============================================================================
8. DECISION RULE
==============================================================================

PROCEED TO PHASE 1 if and only if the 95% bootstrap interval on the primary
endpoint excludes zero AND the Hodges-Lehmann point estimate is at least +10%
per staffed block, over at least 40 coins each contributing at least 1 stratum
in which both arms were observed.

The rule is evaluated mechanically, with no human in the loop, and the
statement it emits is the one committed below, verbatim. There is no branch in
which a result is interpreted, subset or re-analysed against a different
endpoint.

  ON SUCCESS
    DECISION: PROCEED TO PHASE 1. The pre-registered 95% interval on the
    desk-coverage effect excludes zero and the point estimate on
    quote-denominated Venue-pool creator fees clears the committed threshold.

  ON FAILURE
    DECISION: DO NOT PROCEED TO PHASE 1. The pre-registered desk-coverage
    effect was not demonstrated. The operations component, which carries
    roughly 45% of the claimed value of the service, is therefore unsupported
    by this experiment. This result is not to be reinterpreted, subset, or
    re-analysed against a different endpoint; the plan was fixed before the
    data existed and this is the answer it returned.

  IF UNDERPOWERED
    DECISION: DO NOT PROCEED TO PHASE 1 ON THIS EVIDENCE. The study did not
    reach the pre-registered minimum sample, so it neither demonstrates nor
    refutes the desk-coverage effect. Enrol the committed number of coins and
    rerun the same plan; do not analyse the partial sample for a result.

  IF DELIVERY COMPROMISED
    DECISION: DO NOT PROCEED TO PHASE 1 ON THIS EVIDENCE. The desk failed to
    deliver its own assigned arm on more than the pre-registered share of
    staffed blocks, so the arms do not describe what actually happened and the
    contrast is not interpretable in either direction. This is an operator
    failure, not a finding about desks. Fix the delivery problem, rerun the
    trial under a fresh assignment, and report both runs.

==============================================================================

  enabled   40 coins, 14-day control lag
  endpoint fees_log1p   estimator difference_in_differences

Only the ORDER of purchase is randomized; every coin gets the listing. The
sub-study informs procurement and DOES NOT gate Phase 1. The operator buys the
listing as principal, in its own name, out of its own money.

10. WHAT IS PUBLISHED, INCLUDING IF THE RESULT IS NULL
==============================================================================

PUBLICATION COMMITMENT: this pre-registration, the assignment, the per-block
dataset, the exclusion log and the analysis output are published in full
within 30 days of the analysis date, whatever the answer is. A null result is
published with the same prominence as a positive one, under the failure
statement committed above, and the operations component of the service is
withdrawn from the offer rather than re-argued. No endpoint, exclusion,
subgroup or estimator that does not appear in this document will be reported
as though it had been planned.

  Published within 30 days of the analysis date:
    - this pre-registration document and its hash, with the commitment
      timestamp and anchor
    - the published seed and the full block-level assignment, so any third
      party can recompute every arm
    - the per-block dataset: arm, phase, strata, follow window, fee total in
      minor units and USD with its price provenance
    - the exclusion log: every block dropped, under which registered rule,
      with its integrity facts
    - the analysis output verbatim, including the Wilcoxon statistic, the
      Hodges-Lehmann estimate, the bootstrap interval and both sensitivity
      re-runs
    - the registered power analysis and the adequacy statement, unedited

==============================================================================
11. WHAT WOULD INVALIDATE THIS TRIAL
==============================================================================

  - Any change to this plan after commitment. The hash is checked at analysis
    time and a mismatch stops the analysis rather than warning about it.
  - A dataset whose assignment hash does not match the committed assignment:
    the arms may have moved.
  - An exclusion set that differs from the one this plan produces, in either
    direction.
  - Any covariate introduced at analysis time, including a stratification
    variable re-declared as an adjustment.
  - Delivery failure above 10% of staffed blocks, which voids the arm contrast
    and returns COMPROMISED_DELIVERY.
  - A plan committed after the first observation. The analysis checks the
    commitment timestamp against the earliest observation and refuses.
